Cocosations is a free and open source Jabber/XMPP client for Android based on Conversations. It keeps everything Conversations offers and adds tools that show why a call does not connect or sounds bad, a channel check for calls and the last seen time of contacts.

<b>Call diagnostics</b>
• Call setup progress on the call screen: server, federation, contact, media. A failed stage turns red and its reason is shown below
• Call report for each call, available from the chat menu: XMPP errors, signaling delays, client and features of the contact, ICE servers, candidates and pairs, media route (direct or via TURN), NAT type estimate, recommendation and timeline
• Audio quality in both directions: MOS, RTT, packet loss, jitter and bitrate, with charts on the call screen and in the report
• A short tone in the earpiece when the media connection is lost and when it is restored
• Call journal with filters, report export as text or JSON, optional app and WebRTC log for each call

<b>Channel check for calls</b>
Checks the path to your TURN server over UDP, TCP and TLS without calling anyone: a voice-like packet stream goes through the server and back, then delay, jitter, loss and MOS are shown. Useful before a call through a VPN or proxy.

<b>Last seen</b>
Last activity of contacts in the contact list, chat list, chat header and contact details (XEP-0319, XEP-0256, XEP-0012). Publishing your own activity is a separate setting, off by default.

<b>Incoming calls via the system Phone app</b>
Optional: incoming audio calls are shown by the system Phone app, so they can be answered or declined from a smartwatch, headset or car.

<b>Features of Conversations</b>
• End-to-end encryption with OMEMO or OpenPGP
• Send and receive images and other kinds of files
• Encrypted audio and video calls (DTLS-SRTP)
• Share your location
• Send voice messages
• Emoji reactions
• Private group chats and public conferences
• Multiple accounts (unified inbox)
• Synchronizes with desktop clients (Gajim and Dino)
• Very low impact on battery life

<i><b>Note:</b></i> Calls and the channel check need STUN/TURN on your server (XEP-0215). Some other features also require server support.

Cocosations installs next to Conversations: the apps have different application IDs and separate data.
